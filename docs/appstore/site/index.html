What CardScanner does

CardScanner turns a paper business card into an iPhone contact. You photograph the card, or import a photo of one, and the app reads the text on it and sorts that text into fields — name, job title, company, phone, email, website and address. You review those fields and fix anything that looks wrong, then Apple's own New Contact form opens pre-filled so you can save the contact. Both the text recognition and the small language model that structures the text run on your iPhone, using two AI models the app downloads once on first launch; after that download, scanning needs no network connection at all. Every scan is also kept in a local History on the device, which only you can see.

Frequently asked questions

The questions people actually ask, answered plainly.

Why does the first launch download about 530 MB?

Because the reading happens on your phone rather than on a server, the AI has to be on the phone. Those two model files are not included in the App Store download: on first launch the app fetches them once from Hugging Face, a public model host — about 37 MB for the text reader and about 493 MB for the language model. Wi-Fi is recommended. It happens once.

Can that download be paused and resumed?

Yes, and mostly it handles itself. If your connection drops, the download stops and starts again on its own when you are back online; there is also a Resume download button on the setup screen. If the phone is low on free space, it pauses and picks up again once you have freed some and returned to the app. A model file that already finished is kept and is never fetched twice — only a model that was still part-way through has to start over.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once the models are on the device. Scanning, reading, structuring and saving all happen locally, so airplane mode changes nothing — that is the honest test, and the app invites you to try it. Fetching those model files is the only thing CardScanner ever uses the network for.

Which languages of card text can it read?

The on-device reader recognizes English text. Cards printed in other scripts may come back partly or entirely unrecognized. Whatever the reader misses, you can still type into the fields yourself before saving.

Why doesn't the app ask for Contacts permission?

Because it does not need one, and never asks. To save a card, CardScanner hands your reviewed fields to Apple's own New Contact form, pre-filled. That form belongs to iOS and is shown to you, not to the app: iOS presents it without giving CardScanner any access to your address book, and you are the one who taps Save. So the app cannot read, list, search or modify the contacts you already have — iOS enforces that, rather than the app merely promising it. The only permissions CardScanner ever asks for are the camera and the photo library.

It said "Handed to Contacts" — was the contact actually saved?

Only if you tapped Done in the New Contact form that iOS presented. iOS does not tell the app whether you tapped Done or Cancel, so the app genuinely cannot know — which is why the wording is "handed to" rather than "saved". If the contact is not in your Contacts app, open the scan in History and tap Add to Contacts again; nothing was lost.

What if the AI reads a card wrong?

Fix it on the review screen before you save — every field is editable, and the ones the model filled in are marked so you know what to double-check. If a card's layout defeats the model completely, the app falls back to letting you assign the recognized text to fields yourself. The raw text the reader found is kept with the scan in History, so nothing that was on the card is thrown away.

Where are my scans stored?

In the app's own storage area on your iPhone. Each scan keeps the card photo, a thumbnail, the extracted fields, the raw recognized text, and the date. That is your History. It is not uploaded, not synced, and not sent to the developer — there is no server to send it to, and no account to attach it to.

How do I delete a scan, or everything?

Swipe a row in History and tap Delete, or open the scan and use Delete scan. That removes its photo, thumbnail, fields and raw text from the device immediately. To remove everything at once, delete the CardScanner app from your iPhone — iOS removes the app together with all of its stored data, including the models. Contacts you already saved live in your address book, not in CardScanner, so delete those in the Contacts app.

How do I free up space, or delete the models?

Open Settings in the app, tap AI models, and you will see exactly what each model is taking up on this iPhone. Delete either one to reclaim the space; you can download it again later from the same screen. Deleting a model does not touch your History — your scans stay where they are.

Which iPhones and iOS versions are supported?

An iPhone running iOS 17.0 or later, in portrait. There is no iPad-specific version and no Android version. You will also want roughly 530 MB of free space for the models, plus room for the scans you keep.

Privacy

Your card photos, the text read from them and the contact fields stay on your iPhone. There is no account, no server run by the developer, and no advertising, tracking or analytics inside the app. The one thing that does leave the device — a download counter run by the open-source AI library the app is built on, which is told that a model was downloaded — is described in full in the policy.
